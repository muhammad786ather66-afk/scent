Our Philosophy

A fragrance that becomes your signature.

VELORA is a contemporary fragrance house inspired by elegance, confidence, and the art of leaving a lasting impression.

Our fragrances are created for those who see scent as an expression of identity — from the mysterious depth of Midnight, to the electric freshness of Silver Storm, the refined character of Royal Dusk, and the sensual warmth of Noir Vanilla.

Every VELORA fragrance is designed to complement your presence, evoke emotion, and become part of the moments you remember.

Industrial Craft

The 50 ML Signature Vessel

The physical vessel of VELORA is an exercise in sculptural minimalism. Engineered in weighted cosmetic-grade glass with an octagonal silhouette, each bottle reflects light with crystalline clarity.

Crowned with a multi-faceted diamond-cut crystal stopper and adorned with our metallic monogram plate, the bottle is designed to command an elevated position on your dressing table or vanity.
